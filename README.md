# LivePitch AI Desktop

Desktop companion app for **LivePitch AI** — your AI-powered sales call assistant.

## Download

Head over to the [Releases](https://github.com/Synikaa/LivePitchAI_DesktopApp_V3/releases) page to download the latest version for your platform.

### Available Platforms

- **Windows** (.exe installer)
- **macOS** (.dmg) — coming soon

## Installation

### Windows

1. Download the `.exe` installer from the [latest release](https://github.com/Synikaa/LivePitchAI_DesktopApp_V3/releases/latest)
2. Run the installer and follow the setup wizard
3. Launch **LivePitch AI Desktop** from your Start Menu or Desktop shortcut

### macOS

1. Download the `.dmg` file from the [latest release](https://github.com/Synikaa/LivePitchAI_DesktopApp_V3/releases/latest)
2. Open the `.dmg` and drag **LivePitch AI Desktop** to your Applications folder
3. Launch from Applications

## Getting Started

1. Open the app and sign in with your [LivePitch AI](https://www.livepitchai.com) account
2. Select your sales documents and start a new call session
3. Let the AI assist you in real-time with suggestions, talking points, and document-backed answers
4. View your session history and transcripts on the [web dashboard](https://www.livepitchai.com/dashboard)

## Features

- **Real-time AI coaching** — Get instant suggestions on what to say during live sales calls
- **Document-powered answers** — AI retrieves relevant info from your uploaded sales documents
- **Live transcription** — Automatic speech-to-text for both you and your prospect
- **Screen analysis** — Analyze shared screens for context-aware assistance
- **Auto AI mode** — Hands-free suggestions that appear automatically as the conversation flows
- **Transparent overlay** — Stays on top of your video call without getting in the way

## Requirements

- An active [LivePitch AI](https://www.livepitchai.com) account
- Windows 10+ or macOS 12+
- Microphone access
